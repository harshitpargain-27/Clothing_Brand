### The 8 Core Brand Touchpoints

1. **Luxury Shopping Bag:** 320gsm Fedrigoni Materica matte ivory artboard, sculpted blind debossed & champagne gold hot-stamped **Circular Stitched Medallion Seal**, centered `MEEN MANIFESTATION` typography, 14mm round braided warm ivory cotton rope handles.
2. **Premium Rigid Clothing Box:** 1800gsm kappa greyboard wrapped in tactile beige buckram paper (`#EDE8DF`), centered **Circular Stitched Medallion Seal** in champagne gold foil, concealed Neodymium N52 magnetic catch with acoustic snap, lined in ivory velvet with satin pull tab.
3. **Clothing Hang Tags:** Double-sided 650gsm duplex ivory/beige card, beveled champagne gilded edges, **Circular Stitched Medallion Seal** on front; technical garment specs, size matrix (`XS S [M] L XL`), fabric composition (`100% Italian Cashmere / 100% Silk`), and vector authentication QR code on back; attached via ivory waxed cotton cord and engraved brass safety pin.
4. **Clothing Neck Labels:** Ultra-dense 100D/2 damask jacquard woven labels with zero-chafe ultrasonic soft edges. Primary charcoal label with champagne metallic lurex yarn weaving the **Circular Stitched Medallion Seal**; companion ivory silk lining label; companion size loop tabs.
5. **Luxury Thank-You Card:** 450gsm GMUND cotton heavy stock. Front: `MEEN MANIFESTATION` with **Circular Stitched Medallion Seal**. Back: typeset client manifesto quote:
   > *"Thank you for being part of our manifestation.*  
   > *Wear your identity.*  
   > *Manifest your style."*
6. **Archival Monogram Tissue Paper:** 24gsm warm white archival translucent acid-free tissue paper featuring a subtle 45-degree diagonal repeat of the **Circular Stitched Medallion Seal** at 22% opacity in champagne pigment, finished with a 35mm embossed gold foil sticker seal.
7. **Durable Shipping Box:** 200E fluted heavy-duty corrugated shipper in natural ecru/beige kraft with charcoal **Circular Stitched Seal** on outer flap, sealed with 70mm fiberglass-reinforced water-activated paper tape printed with the official brand banner lockup.
8. **Social Media Branding:** Unified Instagram profile aesthetic (`@meenmanifestation`) with **Circular Stitched Seal avatar**, 5 circular highlight badges (Collection, Manifesto, Atelier, Packaging, Press), square 1:1 editorial product launch template, and 9:16 vertical runway story announcement.
