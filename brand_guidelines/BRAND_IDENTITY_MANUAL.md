### 1. Brand DNA & Celestial Fish Symbolism

**MEEN MANIFESTATION** is anchored by the sacred and timeless symbolism of **"Meen"** (the Sanskrit & astrological Pisces fish—an ancient representation of fluidity, intuition, prosperity, and divine creation/manifestation).

In the maison's visual language, the fish is rendered as an ethereal, celestial creature adorned with:
- **Diamond Cross-Hatched Scales:** Representing sacred geometry, precision tailoring, and structured beauty.
- **Graceful Tiered Fins & Radiating Rays:** Flowing lines reflecting the weightlessness of silk and the drape of fine cashmere.
- **Four-Point Sparkle Stars & Bubbles:** Symbolizing cosmic manifestation—turning aspiration into sartorial reality.

> *"Not Just Fashion. A Statement of Identity."*

### 2. Official Brand Architecture

The visual identity is anchored by three official core marks:

#### 2.1 The Official Circular Stitched Medallion Seal (Primary Maison Seal)
- **Replaces the former MM monogram** as the primary circular emblem across all packaging and apparel.
- **Anatomy:**
  - Concentric outer solid border.
  - Intermediate couture dashed stitching line (`stroke-dasharray="7 5"`).
  - Inner hairline ring.
  - Centered Celestial Golden Fish with sparkle stars and floating bubbles.
  - Fine dotted horizontal hairline divider.
  - `MEEN MANIFESTATION` set in classical serif capitals curving across the base.
- **Applications:** Shopping bag front emblem, rigid apparel box center lid seal, hang tag front face, woven collar label, thank-you card front, envelope wax seal, 35mm tissue paper sticker seal, and Instagram profile avatar.

#### 2.2 The Official Brand Banner (Asset 01)
- **Aspect Ratio:** 4 : 1 horizontal banner lockup.
- **Composition:** Ethereal celestial fish line-art on left, centered `MEEN MANIFESTATION` with slogan `NOT JUST FASHION. A STATEMENT OF IDENTITY.`, and subtle golden water reflection below.
- **Applications:** Packaging sealing tape, web hero banner, unboxing box sleeves.

#### 2.3 The Official Primary Logo (Asset 02)
- **Composition:** Standalone Celestial Golden Fish centered directly above horizontal `MEEN MANIFESTATION` serif typography and slogan.
- **Applications:** Garment certificates of authenticity, formal stationery letterheads, collection lookbooks.

### 3. Strict Color System & Finishes

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ivory** | `#F8F7F3` | 248, 247, 243 | 2, 2, 4, 0 | Pantone Warm Gray 1 C (uncoated) | Shopping bags, thank-you cards, tissue base, woven label ground |
| **Champagne** | `#B49A70` | 180, 154, 112 | 20, 30, 60, 15 | Kurz Luxor 429 / Pantone 871 C | Metallic hot foil stamping, fish emblem accents, gilded edges, brass pins |
| **Beige** | `#EDE8DF` | 237, 232, 223 | 5, 5, 10, 2 | Pantone 7527 C | Rigid apparel box wrap, envelope body, shipping box exterior |
| **Charcoal** | `#252525` | 37, 37, 37 | 65, 55, 55, 80 | Pantone Black 7 C | Wordmarks, technical garment specs, woven damask collar label |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | 0, 0, 0, 0 | Pure Opaque / Bleached Silk | Archival tissue ground, silk garment linings, ribbon edge trims |

#### Print & Stamping Finishing Standards:
- **Hot Stamping Foil:** **Kurz Luxor 429 Champagne Gold** (warm satin matte sheen; eliminates brassy reflections).
- **Sculpted Multi-Level Embossing:** 0.35 mm relief for the fish body with razor-sharp micro-embossed scale texture.
- **Varnish:** Zero-solvent aqueous soft-touch matte dispersion coating.

### 5. Complete Asset Specifications (All 8 Touchpoints)

#### 5.1 Luxury Shopping Bag
- **Substrate:** 320gsm Fedrigoni Materica Ivory artboard with aqueous matte protective coating.
- **Branding:** 115mm sculpted **Circular Stitched Medallion Seal** in champagne gold hot foil debossing, centered brand slogan: *"NOT JUST FASHION. A STATEMENT OF IDENTITY."*
- **Handles:** 14mm thick round braided herringbone 100% cotton rope in Warm Ivory (480mm loop), anchored via eyelet-less reinforced 800gsm turn-in inserts.
- **Base:** Reinforced 900gsm duplex base insert board glued securely with archival starch adhesive.

#### 5.2 Premium Rigid Clothing Box

- **Core Board:** 1800gsm Kappa / Eska rigid greyboard (3.0mm thickness).

- **Lid Emblem:** 55mm **Circular Stitched Medallion Seal** foil stamped in champagne gold.

#### 5.3 Double-Sided Clothing Hang Tags

#### 5.4 Woven Neck Labels & Trims

#### 5.5 Luxury Thank-You Card & Envelope

- **Back:** Client manifesto typeset in Italian italic serifs and spaced small caps:
  > *"Thank you for being part of our manifestation.*

### 6. Manufacturing Standards & Quality Control Checklist

| Milestone | Checkpoint | Tolerance Limit | Action on Failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Color Accuracy** | Spectrophotometer delta-E on Ivory (#F8F7F3) and Beige (#EDE8DF) | \(\Delta E \le 1.2\) | Reject batch; recalibrate ink viscosity and water balance. |
| **Foil Stamping** | Crispness of fish scales and circular couture stitching | Sharp edges, zero haloing/flaking | Adjust hot die temperature (125°C ± 3°C) and dwell time (0.4s). |
| **Magnetic Snap** | Clamshell lid retention force | 2.2 kg to 2.6 kg pull | Re-verify neodymium magnet depth and adhesive bonding. |
| **Woven Labels** | Thread count and pick density | Min 140 picks/cm | Reject loose weaves; inspect damask rapier loom tension. |
| **Box Alignment** | Corner mitre alignment and lid squareness | \(\pm 0.5 \text{ mm}\) | Check V-groove routing blades on rigid kappa board. |
